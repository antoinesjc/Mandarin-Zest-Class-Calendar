1. 10 years ago

    Two journeys begin

    In Belgium and in Poland, Antoine and Dorota each sit down with their very first Chinese characters.

  2. 2020

    Friends in Taiwan

    Their paths cross at university in Taiwan, studying in Chinese, and they quickly become good friends.

  3. 2024

    A newsletter in Brussels

    Back in Europe with no Chinese around them, they start a newsletter on learning Chinese to keep practising. Thousands of learners join in.

  4. 2026

    Mandarin Zest is born

    The newsletter grows into a real business: quality learning materials, live speaking sessions and bootcamps.
